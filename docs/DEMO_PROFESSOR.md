# Five-minute prototype demonstration
1. Open the app and enter your name. Point out that no email or password is needed.
2. Name the decision and shortlist two or three products, then click Start Shopping Party to enter the live room.
3. Copy the room invite and open it in three other browser windows/profiles. Enter a different name in each. For a phone, use a reachable address on the same Wi-Fi with the exact allowed origin configured; a localhost URL cannot reach another device.
4. Change the product. Show all the other screens changing together. Ask friends to react and comment or chat.
5. Open Party controls and switch browsing to Everyone browses freely. Let a guest browse and suggest a product, then bring everyone to it. Transfer control to a guest and have them change the product.
6. Open Compare & ask and ask VYUMI to compare the two real catalog entries. Create a vote split and show its disagreement note. Missing details stay unknown.
7. The current host ends the party. Show the honest winner/tie, stats, downloadable image, public result page and revoke action.

Fallback: keep the demo on localhost in four independent browser contexts/windows. Existing saved rooms expire; create a fresh room before presenting. Use built-in catalog items when URL previews are blocked. No merchant purchase, model setup, external account or video call is needed. Do not claim the host panel is a general AI model, that catalog prices are current offers, or that the prototype is deployed for production.
